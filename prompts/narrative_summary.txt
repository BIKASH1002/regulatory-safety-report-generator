# Narrative Summary and Analysis

Generate a brief opening narrative (3-4 paragraphs) that summarizes the safety profile of the product based on the supplied evidence.

## Reporting Period
{reporting_period}

## Approved Evidence
- Total unique cases: {total_cases}
- Serious cases: {serious_cases} ({serious_percentage}%)
- Non-serious cases: {non_serious_cases}
- Reporting period span: {period_start} to {period_end}
- Most frequent reactions: {top_reactions}
- Geographic distribution: primarily {primary_countries}

## Your Task
Write a concise narrative that:
1. Describes the volume and nature of reports received during the period
2. Notes the proportion classified as serious
3. Identifies the most commonly reported reactions
4. Acknowledges geographic or temporal patterns in the data
5. Does NOT claim safety signals, causality, or product efficacy
6. Does NOT reference unavailable data (e.g., SOC, expectedness, clinical outcomes)

Keep tone neutral and regulatory. Reference only the evidence above.
